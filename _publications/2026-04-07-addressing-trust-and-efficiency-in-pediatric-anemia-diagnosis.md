Pediatric anemia is a common health problem that may result in poor cognitive development, exhaustion, and irreversible developmental deficiencies if not diagnosed and treated. This work proposes an optimized Decision Tree for classifying pediatric anemia, with a focus on interpretability and computational efficiency. A two-step feature selection approach combining statistical tests and a Genetic Algorithm is used to identify informative features, including Hemoglobin (Hb) and Packed Cell Volume (PCV). The hyperparameters are optimized using GridSearch to improve the model's performance and reliability.
