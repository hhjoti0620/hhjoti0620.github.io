I am a final-year undergraduate student in Computer Science and Telecommunication Engineering at Noakhali Science and Technology University, Bangladesh, expected to graduate in October 2026. My academic and research interests are focused on Artificial Intelligence for healthcare, particularly Computer Vision, Medical Image Analysis, and Deep Learning.

My current undergraduate thesis, **“MedRxAI: A Deep Learning-Based Framework for Handwritten Prescription Analysis and Medication Recommendation in Bangladesh,”** focuses on applying machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision techniques to handwritten prescription images and medication-related analysis. Alongside my thesis, I am conducting an individual research project, **“MorphAI: Multi-Variant Analysis of Goldenhar Syndrome Using Heterogeneous Medical Images,”** exploring deep learning and medical image analysis.

I am currently seeking **Master’s opportunities in Computer Science**, particularly in **Computer Vision, Healthcare AI, and Medical AI**. I am also interested in research-oriented opportunities and Research Assistant positions where I can contribute to research labs working on AI-driven healthcare and medical imaging.
